## Mission

FLOW is the operating system for the modern enterprise.

It ingests every signal from every connected tool, classifies it, reasons about it, and surfaces the three decisions your company needs to make right now — in under 60 seconds.

---

## Vision

In ten years, every company with more than ten people runs on an operating system. Not a collection of SaaS tools. Not a dashboard. An operating system: a single plane where work is understood, prioritized, and executed.

FLOW is that operating system.

Where email, calendar, code, Slack, CRM, HR, and documents once lived in seventeen separate tabs, FLOW reads them all, reasons across all of them, and speaks with one voice: a Chief of Staff who has already read everything and knows exactly what needs to happen next.

---

## What FLOW Is

**An enterprise operating system** that continuously ingests, classifies, and reasons about every communication signal, work artifact, and business event across an organization.

**A decision surface**, not a dashboard. Every screen answers a single question: what should you do next?

**A governed execution layer** that can take approved actions on your behalf — merging PRs, sending approvals, filing issues, scheduling meetings — through a risk-tiered governance pipeline.

**A persistent intelligence layer** that learns the shape of your company: who owns what, what is at risk, what has been decided, and what is about to happen.

**A trust-first platform** that operates on deny-by-default access. FLOW never reads a calendar, channel, or repository without explicit user permission. Every connected source is visible and controllable.

---

## What FLOW Is Not

FLOW is not a chatbot. It does not wait to be asked.

FLOW is not a dashboard. It does not present data for users to interpret.

FLOW is not an automation platform. Every action requires governance — DENY, CONFIRM, APPROVE, or TWO-PERSON — and every outcome is audited.

FLOW is not a replacement for Slack, GitHub, Jira, or Gmail. It reads them. It reasons across them. It surfaces what matters. The source tools remain the record of truth.

FLOW is not a feature set. It is an operating system. Features do not ship unless they fit the operating model. Adding a feature that bypasses the intelligence pipeline, ignores governance, or cannot be explained is not shipping — it is adding clutter.

## North Star

> A CTO opens FLOW at 7:45 AM. Without configuring anything, they see three things that matter. They handle all three by 8:10 AM. The rest of their day is better because of it.

This is the north star. Every decision — navigation, AI behavior, design, API design, engineering rule — is measured against this moment.

**Corollary:** If a feature makes the CTO's first 60 seconds worse, it does not ship.

## Product Principles

### 1. Decisions before data
FLOW surfaces what to decide, not what has happened. A recommendation with context is worth more than a chart with no action.

### 2. Execution before analytics
FLOW completes work. Every recommendation ends in an action. Every action is tracked. Insights without execution are observations, not intelligence.

### 3. Trust before intelligence
FLOW only reasons about what you have explicitly permitted it to see. Trust is not a setting. It is the foundation. A user who does not trust the data source cannot trust the intelligence derived from it.

### 4. Chief of Staff, not assistant
FLOW speaks with authority and context. It does not wait to be asked. It does not hedge. It has already read everything. It tells you what matters.

### 5. Enterprise-grade by default
Multi-tenant isolation, role-based access, governance at every action, audit logs at every event. These are not features — they are preconditions. FLOW is safe to deploy in regulated industries, at sensitive companies, for executive decision-making.

### 6. Invisible when unnecessary
FLOW does not interrupt. The Command Center is always available but never demands attention. Notifications are reserved for decisions that cannot wait. Everything else is there when you need it.

### 7. Every capability must be discoverable
Navigation is a product decision. Burying a feature in settings is product failure. Every capability FLOW provides — integrations, governance, audit, AI memory, execution — must be reachable from the sidebar within two clicks.
